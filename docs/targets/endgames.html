Decisions you will see

  • Menu gets one new button, "Practice an endgame", under "Set up a position".
  • Endgames screen: search box, a random button, and five families with counts.
  • Family screen: positions grouped by material, each with a Win or Draw tag and side to move.
  • Position screen: the board is shown before you start, so you can memorize it.
  • The piece list is also written out in text, the way you would hear it read.
  • You always play the side to move. Your White/Black setting does not apply here.
  • "Play Blindfold" uses the same label as the setup screen.
  • Each position names its source in small text.
  • A position with no known name shows as "Position N" inside its group.
  • Typing in search replaces the family list with matching positions. Each match shows its group and side to move.
  • No matches shows one line and a "Clear search" button.
  • "Play Blindfold" and the random buttons are disabled while the engine loads, same as the setup screen.
  • Game over in an endgame: "Try again" replays the same position. "Another from this group" picks a random one. "Endgames" returns to the catalog. Copy PGN is unchanged.
  • The game-over panel adds one line under the result: "Goal was a win." or "Goal was a draw."
  • In an endgame, the "New Game" button in the More sheet reads "Try again" and replays the position.
  • Normal games and set-up positions keep today's panel and buttons exactly.
  • Board pieces and the menu logo here are stand-ins. The real build uses the app's own assets.
