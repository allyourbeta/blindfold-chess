Decisions you will see

  • The label style you pick applies to every board in the app: endgame preview, set-up screen, and the peek board during a game.
  • L1: each row gets a small board on the left, drawn from the side you would play.
  • L3: the list becomes a grid of boards, two per row, each with its number, side to move and piece count underneath.
  • L2: rows unchanged; holding a row shows a large preview until you let go.
