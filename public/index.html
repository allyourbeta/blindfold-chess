How to Play

Type moves in standard algebraic notation:

e4 — pawn to e4

Nf3 — knight to f3

Bxe5 — bishop captures on e5

O-O — castle kingside

e8=Q — pawn promotes to queen

peek or Spacebar — see the board for 3 seconds
hint — show legal moves
history — view past games & stats

🎤 Voice: say moves naturally, or use NATO letters (Alpha, Bravo, Charlie...)
